# Indian E-Commerce Analytics

End-to-end Data Analytics project using Excel, PostgreSQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze 2,500 e-commerce transactions and uncover actionable business insights.

---

# Project Overview

This project analyzes an Indian E-commerce dataset containing 2,500 transactions across multiple product categories, states, payment methods, sales channels, 
and customer demographics.

The objective was to identify revenue drivers, profitability trends, customer behavior patterns, geographic performance, and operational efficiency metrics 
to support business decision-making.

# Key Business Insights

### Revenue Concentration

* Electronics contributed 53.53% of total revenue.
* Top 3 categories generated over 87% of total revenue.

### Geographic Performance

* Maharashtra generated the highest revenue.
* Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Andhra Pradesh contributed approximately 36% of total revenue.

### Customer Insights

* Customers aged 45–54 generated the highest revenue.
* Revenue distribution is highly concentrated among a small group of customers.

### Payment Analysis

* UPI contributed more than 45% of total revenue.
* Digital payments dominated customer transactions.

### Product Performance

* Laptop was the highest revenue-generating product.
* Laptop was also the highest profit-generating product.

### Operational Insights

* Average customer rating: 4.02/5
* Average delivery time: 3.52 days
* Delivered order rate: 74.88%

# Business Recommendations

1. Reduce revenue dependency on Electronics by expanding high-margin categories.
2. Increase investment in Mobile App acquisition and retention strategies.
3. Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing states.
4. Improve return management processes to reduce operational losses.
5. Promote high-margin product categories through targeted campaigns.
